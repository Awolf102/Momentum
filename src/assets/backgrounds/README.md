# Built-in backgrounds

Drop photographs into this folder (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp` or `.avif`)
and they become built-in backgrounds the next time the app builds.

- The name shown in the app comes from the file name:
  `australia-mountains.jpg` → **Australia Mountains**.
- The first photo (alphabetically) becomes the default background.
- For fast loading, export at around 2560 px on the long edge, JPEG quality ~85.

Photos added here are committed to the repository and bundled with the app.
To keep a photo private to one browser instead, add it from
**Settings → Appearance → Your photo**.

## Rewards

Photos in `unlocks/<days>/` (for example `unlocks/30/great-ocean-road.jpg`)
stay locked until your longest streak reaches that many days, then appear in
the picker with a celebration.
